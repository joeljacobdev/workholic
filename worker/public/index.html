          Let a script on the Mac push it later

          On each Mac, put an executable script in ~/Library/Application Support/tech.joeljacob.workholic/commands/. It prints one time, 21:30 or 2026-10-17T21:30:00-07:00; if that is later, the Mac waits for it.

          Breaks

          A session is the time you spend looking at a screen between breaks. You never start one. It begins when you sit down and ends when you step away. A break is the prompt to step away: the screen goes dark until it is over.

          Every few minutes

            Past the daily limit

            Past the limit, this replaces the every-few-minutes pause, so sessions get shorter. The first pause comes that many minutes after you cross it. Nothing changes before the limit.

            At set times

              Covers the screen from that time for its length, once a day, in each Mac’s local time. “5 more minutes” puts it off.

              Breaks run in the Mac app, on each Mac by itself. Time on another device during a break still counts toward today. Changes reach every Mac and browser within a few minutes, or at once when you open the Mac's menu; if two places change breaks, the later save wins. Every automatic break offers “5 more minutes”; you can also start one from the Mac’s menu bar.

              This Mac

              macOS needs your OK: turn Workholic on in System Settings › General › Login Items.

              Pause keeps the Mac awake and your programs running behind a dark screen. Locked, getting back in asks for your Mac password or Touch ID, and switching apps or quitting is blocked until then.

              These stay on this Mac.
