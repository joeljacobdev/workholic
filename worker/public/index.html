    Daily limit

    A new limit applies from today onward. Past days keep the limit they had.

    Breaks

    A session is the time you spend looking at a screen between breaks. You never start one. It begins when you sit down and ends when you step away. A break is the prompt to step away: the screen goes dark until it is over.

    Every few minutes

      Past the daily limit

      Past the limit, this replaces the every-few-minutes pause, so sessions get shorter. The first pause comes that many minutes after you cross it. Nothing changes before the limit.

      At set times

        Covers the screen from that time for its length, once a day, in each Mac’s local time. “5 more minutes” puts it off.

        Breaks run in the Mac app, on each Mac by itself. Time on another device during a break still counts toward today. Changes reach every Mac and browser within about 10 seconds; if two places change breaks, the later save wins. Every automatic break offers “5 more minutes”; you can also start one from the Mac’s menu bar.
